# Food Scanner Privacy Policy

DRAFT for legal review before publication. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Publisher: FutureSpace. Contact: food.productscanner@gmail.com

## Summary

Food Scanner reads product barcodes and compares the listed ingredients with dietary profiles you create. Your profiles and history stay on your device. We do not operate a server, run accounts, use analytics or advertising, or track you.

## Information stored on your device

- Dietary profiles and preferences
- Scan history and favourites
- Cached product details
- App settings

This information is not sent to us or to any third party. You can clear history, cache and favourites in Settings, and a full reset removes all saved data. Deleting the app removes everything.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Frames are processed on the device and are not saved or sent anywhere. Camera access is optional; you can type a barcode instead. You can change the permission in your device Settings.

## Information sent to third parties

When you look up a product, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) and receives product data in return. The request includes the app name and version and a contact address in its User-Agent header. Open Food Facts also receives your IP address, as any web service does, and may serve product images from its own servers. It handles this under its own privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Your profiles, preferences, history and favourites are never included in these requests.

If you use the Share action, the content you share is passed to the app you choose. That app's own policy applies.

## Data sources and licences

Product data is © Open Food Facts contributors and available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Product images are available under CC BY-SA. Data may be incomplete or out of date.

## Health and safety notice

Results only compare listed ingredients with the active profile. "No profile matches" is not a guarantee that a product is safe. For allergies or medical dietary needs, always check the packaging and consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
